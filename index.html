Zone & Capacity chip → drilldown (not direct action)
Assurance Strip chips are metric tiles. Like every other chip, "Zone & Capacity" now opens a drilldown first — showing zone demand context, current capacity breakdown, and a "Capacity बढ़ाइए" CTA that enters the State A → B → home chain. Matches the Dashboard's established drilldown pattern.

IMPROVEMENT v5 17 May 2026
10-directive design correction sweep · architecture lock
Growth ≠ NetBox ordering. Capacity entry moved out of NetBox into Assurance Strip (new 4th "Zone & Capacity" chip on Dashboard home). NetBox is operational-only (custody / returns / recovery / liabilities). CSP feels they ask to grow business, not buy routers.
Plus 9 more directives: bucket separation in dues (Operational vs Security settlement), no source-reveal at liability stage, "उपलब्ध अतिरिक्त सीमा" instead of cushion, "Settlement Cycle" instead of "हर 3 महीने", "system manages capacity" framing, late recovery "back in process", dispute pause clarity.

AMENDMENT v2 17 May 2026
Settlement waterfall अब loss_category के हिसाब से split है

क्या बदला
पहले (Amendment v1)
Single waterfall: हर liability पर wallet → future payouts → SD backstop।
अब (Amendment v2)
Split waterfall — controllability से तय:
Custody loss / Damage (पूरा control) — wallet first → future payouts → SD backstop. (पहले जैसा।)
Non-recovery (आंशिक control) — SD adjustment सीधे। Wallet को कभी नहीं छूता। (नया।)
क्यों
SD-3 (controllability determines liability) के तहत:
Different controllability → different AMOUNTS (Compensation OS)
Different controllability → different SETTLEMENT PATHS (यह amendment)
Different controllability → different SEVERITY ORDERING (custody first)
Reasoning:
₹200 छोटी रकम है। Non-recovery पर wallet hit करना disproportionate। SD adjustment "collateral erosion" है — दिखता है but wallet pain नहीं। अगर SD minimum तक पहुंचने लगे → top-up escalation, कभी silent wallet overflow नहीं।
